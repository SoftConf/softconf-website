Devoxx Greece 2026 Concludes with Record Attendance

With an impressive energy and attendance that surpassed all previous records, Devoxx Greece 2026, the largest software development conference in Greece, came to a close. From April 23 to 25, the Athens Concert Hall was transformed into an international technology hub, welcoming more than 1,500 attendees from 30 different countries.

This year’s program once again set the bar high, focusing on the practical application of technologies that are redefining software engineering. More than 85 international speakers shared their expertise across a wide range of topics, including programming languages, security, soft skills, artificial intelligence (AI), and more.

The conference agenda was graced by internationally recognized speakers such as Will Sentance, Gojko Adjic, and J. B. Rainsberger, who inspired the audience with their keynote speeches. The conference’s success wasn’t limited to the talks. The workshops, community corner, and mentorship hub gave participants the opportunity for hands-on training in tools that are shaping the future of their work.

"This year’s Devoxx Greece proved that the Greek tech community is stronger than ever. The energy we experienced at the Athens Concert Hall was unique. Our goal remains to continuously improve the experience we offer, bringing global knowledge to our country. Our journey toward 2026 continues with our next major event, Voxxed Days Thessaloniki, on November 20–21, 2026. As always, it will take place at the Ioannis Vellidis Conference Center and will mark our 10th anniversary."

— Patroklos Papapetrou, Organizer of Devoxx Greece & Voxxed Days
