The Voxxed Days Crete conference was a resounding success in Heraklion, Crete

For the first time, Heraklion, Crete, hosted the Voxxed Days Crete conference, one of the largest software development conferences in Greece. The conference took place at the Heraklion Conference and Cultural Center on September 26–27, 2025.

Voxxed Days Crete was a two-day event that drew participants from around the world, attracting industry professionals, students, and experts from Greece and abroad. Renowned speakers from Europe, Asia, and the Americas—who are shaping and supporting the future of software development—actively participated, offering valuable insights and innovative ideas. Particular emphasis was placed on topics related to the latest developments in artificial intelligence, programming languages, soft skills, case studies, software development methodologies, and much more.

"Our decision to expand the Voxxed Days conferences beyond Athens and Thessaloniki and bring the conference to Crete proved to be extremely successful. Crete has a dynamic and active tech community, and Voxxed Days Crete was a great opportunity for professionals and students to exchange knowledge and experiences. Our next stop is Voxxed Days Thessaloniki, which will take place at the Ioannis Vellidis Conference Center in Thessaloniki on November 7–8."

— Patroklos Papapetrou, Organizer of Devoxx Greece & Voxxed Days
