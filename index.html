About Us

Why Us?

Based in Thessaloniki, Greece, SoftConf was founded in 2016 by one of the main local community leaders and ex-organizer of the Devit Conference. We are driven by community demands and are always seeking to provide the latest content and trends in the software industry.

We are dedicated to organizing high-quality conferences, workshops, and other software development-related events such as hackathons, code retreats, and coder dojos. Additionally, our highly respected software professionals provide consulting services that aim to improve our clients' software development processes and the overall quality of their products.

Past Events & History

4 Editions (2023 - 2026) | Athens

Devoxx Greece History

Read the story +

What started as Voxxed Days Athens in 2017 with 450 attendees, evolved into Devoxx Greece in 2023. By 2025, it gathered a record-breaking 1,350+ delegates and 60+ global speakers. Patroklos and the team aimed to create a central tech hub featuring world-class experts, making it the largest tech event in the country.

9 Past Editions (2016 - 2025) | Thessaloniki

Voxxed Days SKG History

Read the story +

Starting in 2016, Voxxed Days Thessaloniki has been the premier tech hub of the Balkans, consistently hosting 40+ sessions and uniting over 700 passionate developers annually. Its massive success proved the immense thirst for knowledge and innovation in Northern Greece, sparking the expansion of the events.

2 Editions (2024 - 2025) | Ioannina

Voxxed Days Ioannina History

Read the story +

It all began in February 2024. The rapid technological growth of Ioannina, combined with its outstanding University, made it the ideal destination. The goal is to strengthen the local tech community in Epirus, connect students with top companies, and retain brilliant engineering talent in the region.

Started in 2025 | Heraklion

Voxxed Days Crete History

Read the story +

The newest member of the family! Scheduled in Heraklion, it was created to connect the island's developers with the global software community. The event highlights Crete as a dynamic new tech hub, bridging academic research with the fast-paced software industry.
