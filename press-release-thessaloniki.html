Voxxed Days Thessaloniki 2025 Concludes with Resounding Success

Voxxed Days Thessaloniki 2025, the region’s most important software development conference, which took place on November 7 and 8, 2025, concluded with resounding success. For two days, the heart of the city’s tech community beat at the “Ioannis Vellidis” conference center, which hosted this year’s Voxxed Days Thessaloniki. This year’s event brought together ~800 participants from 15 countries, making it perhaps the most successful to date.

This year’s program stood out once again for the high quality of its talks and workshops, featuring more than 40 presentations across 10 thematic tracks, covering contemporary topics such as artificial intelligence, big data, new programming languages, and much more.

Two new features that made their debut at the conference drew particular interest. The Community Corner, which hosted some of the city’s most active tech communities, and the Mentorship Hub—which operated on a trial basis for one day—were embraced by the audience and offered valuable mentoring and skill-building opportunities to participants.

"We are particularly pleased with the response and energy generated by this year’s Voxxed Days Thessaloniki. The participation and eagerness to learn from all attendees confirm that our tech community is vibrant and dynamic. We look forward to seeing you in November 2026 to celebrate together the 10th anniversary of the first Voxxed Days Thessaloniki."

— Patroklos Papapetrou, Organizer of Devoxx Greece & Voxxed Days
