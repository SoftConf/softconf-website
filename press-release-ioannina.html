Voxxed Days Expands to Ioannina for its Inaugural Tech Conference

The huge success of Voxxed Days Thessaloniki led the organizers to hold the conference this year, for the first time, in another city that is of great interest in the tech sector. Specifically, Voxxed Days—the largest conference in the technology and software development sector—will be held for the first time in the city of Ioannina on February 23, at the “Karolos Papoulias” Conference Center of the University of Ioannina.

It is worth noting that Voxxed Days is a series of technology conferences based on the philosophy of Devoxx, which takes place in several cities around the world, attracting thousands of participants.

As with every Voxxed Days conference, a host of speakers will share their valuable knowledge with participants. Conference attendees will learn about the latest developments in software development, while also having the opportunity to network and exchange ideas with other colleagues from Greece and abroad.

"We decided to organize Voxxed Days in another city in our country to give audiences from the rest of Greece the opportunity to attend. We chose the city of Ioannina because it is of great technological interest. Major IT companies are based in the city, and the Department of Informatics and Telecommunications at the University of Ioannina were key reasons for our choice. The philosophy of Voxxed Days Ioannina is based on the central idea of the conference, which is none other than acquiring technological knowledge and serving as a meeting place for networking."

— Patroklos Papapetrou, Organizer of Devoxx Greece & Voxxed Days
